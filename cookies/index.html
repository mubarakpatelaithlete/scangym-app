Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This policy explains how LighterMe (lighterme.co.uk) uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you use the site.

Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the site to function. They include:

  • Cookie consent preference — remembers whether you've accepted or rejected cookies

These cannot be disabled as they are essential for the site to work.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

If you consent, we may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. These cookies collect anonymised data about:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • How you arrived at our site
  • Your browser and device type

Affiliate Cookies (Optional)

When you click an affiliate link to a clinic, the affiliate network (Awin) may set a cookie to track the referral. This cookie:

  • Typically lasts 30 days
  • Allows the clinic to attribute your sign-up to our referral
  • Does not collect personal health data

How to Manage Cookies

You can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Cookie banner — when you first visit our site, you can choose to accept or reject non-essential cookies
  • Browser settings — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings
  • Opt-out tools — Google provides an opt-out browser plugin for Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Changes to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
