## Full Description
ScanGym is the world's first universal gym day pass marketplace.

Find, compare, and book gym sessions across 1.2M+ gyms in 190+ countries. No monthly memberships, no contracts — just flexible pay-per-visit access.

🏋️ Key Features:
• Search 1.2M+ gyms worldwide
• Day passes from £4.49 / $5.49
• QR code gym entry — no front desk needed
• AI fitness coach and workout planner
• Real-time pricing and availability
• GPS-based gym finder with filters
• Built-in workout music player
• Creator programme — earn by sharing

How it works:
1. Search for gyms by location
2. Compare prices, ratings, and photos
3. Book a day pass instantly
4. Show your QR code at the gym door
5. Work out — no membership needed!

Available on Galaxy Watch: Check nearby gym availability from your wrist.
