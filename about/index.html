About LighterMe

We're on a mission to help UK women navigate the world of weight management with confidence and clarity.

Our Mission

Finding the right weight management programme shouldn't be confusing, overwhelming, or risky. Too many people fall for unregulated products, misleading marketing, or programmes that aren't backed by real medical expertise.

LighterMe exists to cut through the noise. We research, verify, and compare UK clinician-led weight management programmes so you can make an informed choice with confidence.

We believe that effective weight management starts with the right information — and the right medical support.

How We Make Money

We believe in complete transparency. LighterMe is funded through affiliate partnerships. When you click a link to a clinic on our site and sign up for their programme, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

This is how we keep the site free and independent. Importantly:

  • Our comparisons are honest and unbiased
  • We only feature clinics we've verified as legitimate and regulated
  • Commission rates never influence our recommendations
  • You always pay the same price — our commission comes from the clinic, not you

For full details, see our Affiliate Disclosure.
