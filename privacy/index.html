Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

LighterMe ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit lighterme.co.uk.

1. Who We Are

LighterMe is an affiliate comparison website for UK clinician-led weight management programmes. We are not a medical provider and do not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, or prescribe treatments.

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following data:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, referral source, browser type, device type (collected via cookies if you consent)
  • Contact data — if you contact us via email, we may collect your name and email address

We do not collect sensitive health data. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use this site.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To improve our website and content
  • To understand how visitors use our site (analytics)
  • To respond to your enquiries

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we process your data based on:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies (you can accept or reject via our cookie banner)
  • Legitimate interest — to improve our site and respond to enquiries

5. Cookies

We use cookies to analyse site traffic and improve your experience. You can accept or reject cookies when you first visit our site. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site contains affiliate links to third-party clinics (SheMed, Boots Online Doctor, eMed, WeightWorld, Pharmacy2U). When you click these links, you leave our site and are subject to the third party's privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share anonymised usage data with:

  • Google Analytics (if you consent to analytics cookies)
  • Our affiliate network (Awin) for tracking referrals — this is cookie-based and anonymised

8. Data Retention

We retain usage data for up to 26 months. Contact data is retained for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, then deleted.

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk)

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at: privacy@lighterme.co.uk

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
