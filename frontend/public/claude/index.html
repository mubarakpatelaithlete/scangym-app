Questions

Do I need to pay for anything to connect?

Connecting ScanGym to Claude is free. You only pay when you actually book a gym day pass, at the price shown.

I can’t find “Add custom connector”.

Make sure you’re on the Connectors page in Claude’s settings (use the orange button above). On phones, scroll to the bottom of that page — the button sits under your existing connectors.

Can Claude book and take my money automatically?

No. Claude can search gyms, show prices and hold a provisional slot, but it always asks you to confirm before anything is booked. You can cancel free up to 2 hours before your session.

Is my ScanGym account linked?

Bookings you confirm through Claude appear in your normal ScanGym account, so you can manage or cancel them in the app as usual.
