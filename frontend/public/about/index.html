What is ScanGym?

ScanGym is a gym booking marketplace — like Airbnb, but for gyms. Instead of committing to one gym with a monthly membership, ScanGym lets you buy a single-visit day pass at any gym, anywhere in the world.

Whether you're traveling, trying a new gym, or just hate being locked into contracts — ScanGym gives you the freedom to work out wherever you want, whenever you want.

We aggregate over 1.2 million gyms globally using Google Maps data, so if a gym exists, it's probably on ScanGym.
