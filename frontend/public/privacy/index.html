Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026 · Applies to scangym.com, the ScanGym mobile and desktop apps, and the ScanGym app for ChatGPT.

1. Who we are

ScanGym ("we", "us") operates a gym discovery and day-pass booking marketplace at scangym.com. For UK GDPR purposes, ScanGym is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: hello@scangym.com.

3. The ScanGym app for ChatGPT

When you use ScanGym inside ChatGPT:

  • Inputs we receive: your gym search queries, coordinates if you share them, and, when you make a booking, the name, email address, date and time you provide.
  • Outputs we return: gym information (names, addresses, prices, hours, ratings), booking confirmations with a booking code, and a payment link.
  • Payments never happen inside ChatGPT. Payment is completed on scangym.com via Stripe.
  • We receive these inputs from OpenAI in order to answer your request; we do not receive your ChatGPT conversation history. Your use of ChatGPT itself is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Data received via ChatGPT is used only to provide the search and booking service, it is retained and deletable exactly like data submitted on scangym.com (see sections 5 and 6).

4. Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with processors needed to run the service:

  • Stripe - payment processing and refunds
  • Twilio - SMS login codes and booking notifications
  • Google Maps / Places - gym search and map data
  • OpenAI - powering the AI chat and coach features (inputs to those features only)
  • Supabase - our database hosting
  • Railway - application hosting
  • Cloudflare - content delivery and media storage
  • Gyms you book - the gym receives the booking details needed to admit you (name, booking code, date/time)

We may also disclose data where required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account
  • Bookings and payment records: 6 years (UK financial record-keeping requirements)
  • Usage analytics: up to 24 months
  • Reviews: until you delete them or your account
  • NPS and support messages: up to 24 months

6. Your rights and controls

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and you can object to or restrict certain processing. Email hello@scangym.com and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Guest bookings can be cancelled with your booking code; free cancellation applies up to 2 hours before your session and paid bookings are refunded automatically.

7. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and to remember guest bookings. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Children

ScanGym is for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
