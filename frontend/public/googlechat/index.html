Questions

I can’t find ScanGym in the Apps list.

Work and school Google accounts only see apps their administrator has allowed. Ask your admin to allow “ScanGym”, or use ScanGym on Telegram or in the app instead — same bot, same bookings.

Does it cost anything?

Adding ScanGym to Google Chat is free. You only pay when you book a gym day pass, at the price shown.

Will my bookings show in the ScanGym app?

Yes — anything you book in Google Chat appears in your normal ScanGym account, so you can manage or cancel it as usual.
