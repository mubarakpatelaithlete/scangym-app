Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

We want every ScanGym purchase to feel safe. If something goes wrong, email bookings@scangym.com with your booking code or order and we will sort it out. This policy does not affect your statutory rights under UK consumer law.

1. Gym bookings and passes

  • Free cancellation up to 2 hours before your session. Cancel in the app or any ScanGym chat.
  • Refunds go instantly to your ScanGym Wallet, or back to your card in 5–10 working days.
  • If the gym is closed, refuses entry, or the booking cannot be honoured, you get a full refund.
  • Cancellations less than 2 hours before the session, or no-shows, are not normally refunded.

2. Digital Shop products (PDFs, prompt packs, templates)

  • Digital products are delivered instantly. By downloading, you agree the download starts straight away, so the usual 14-day cancellation right ends once the download has started (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).
  • You still get a full refund if the file is faulty, will not open, or is not as described. Tell us within 30 days of purchase.
  • If you have not downloaded the product yet, you can ask for a refund within 14 days.
  • Duplicate purchases of the same product are refunded in full.

3. Create (AI images, video, audio)

  • If a creation fails and you were charged, email us and we refund that charge.
  • Finished creations are not refunded because the computing cost has already been spent, but tell us if a result is clearly broken and we will review it.

4. Invoices and allowances

  • If you were charged twice, or charged when you still had allowance left, we refund the extra charge.

5. How to ask for a refund

Email bookings@scangym.com with your name, the email or phone on your account, and the booking code or order. We reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method or your ScanGym Wallet.
