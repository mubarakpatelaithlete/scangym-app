Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: May 2026

In the interest of full transparency, this page explains how LighterMe earns revenue and how it affects (and doesn't affect) our content.

How We Earn Revenue

LighterMe is an affiliate comparison website. We earn commission through affiliate partnerships with verified UK weight management clinics and providers.

When you click a link on our site to a clinic and sign up for their programme, the clinic may pay us a referral commission through the Awin affiliate network. This is how we fund the site and keep it free for you to use.

What This Means for You

  • You never pay more — our affiliate commission comes from the clinic, not from you. You pay the same price whether you use our link or go directly to the clinic.
  • Our comparisons are honest — commission rates do not influence our recommendations or the order in which clinics appear on our site.
  • We only feature verified clinics — every clinic on our site has been researched and verified as legitimate, regulated, and clinician-led.

Which Links Are Affiliate Links?

All links to external clinics on our site (including SheMed, Boots Online Doctor, eMed, WeightWorld, and Pharmacy2U) are affiliate links. These are identifiable by the "awin" domain in the URL.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing honest, unbiased information. Our editorial content is independent of our commercial relationships. If we don't think a clinic meets our standards, we won't feature it — regardless of the commission on offer.

Compliance

This disclosure is made in compliance with the UK Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) guidelines and the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008.
